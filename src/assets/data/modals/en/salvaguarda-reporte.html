How to report a concern

If you identify a risk situation, rights violations, inappropriate conduct, or suspected abuse involving children and adolescents, please use the official public safety channels in Pernambuco.

  • Emergência policial (PMPE): 190
  • Disque Denúncia (SDS/PE): 181
  • Delegacia pela Internet (PCPE): https://servicos.sds.pe.gov.br/delegacia/
  • Delegacia Interativa (PCPE): +55 (81) 3184-1155 / +55 (81) 9 9488-7039 / delegaciainterativa@policiacivil.pe.gov.br

In immediate risk situations, call 190. For anonymous reports and suspicions, use 181 and Polícia Civil channels.

Important: whenever possible, record the date, location, factual description, and evidence to support police follow-up.
