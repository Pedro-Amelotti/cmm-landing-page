How to report a concern

If you identified a risk situation, rights violation, inappropriate conduct, or suspected abuse involving children and adolescents, please use the official public safety channels in Pernambuco.

  • Police emergency (PMPE): 190
  • Anonymous Hotline (SDS/PE): 181
  • Online Police Report (PCPE): https://servicos.sds.pe.gov.br/delegacia/
  • Interactive Police Office (PCPE): +55 (81) 3184-1155 / +55 (81) 9 9488-7039 / delegaciainterativa@policiacivil.pe.gov.br

In immediate risk situations, call 190. For anonymous reports and suspicions, use 181 and Civil Police channels.

Important: whenever possible, record date, place, factual description, and evidence to support police referral.
